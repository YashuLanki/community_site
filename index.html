Our Story · Est. 2024

Who we are.

Founded in 2024, Arizona Marshallese Community is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit that represents and supports Marshallese families across the state. Most of us are in the Phoenix area, with families as far out as Tucson and Flagstaff, and many came here for work, school, or medical care.

AMC is run entirely by Marshallese people for our own community. That means helping with things like updating an I-94, signing up for health insurance, applying for scholarships, and pointing families toward local resources, all while keeping our language and culture close.

That's what family means to us. We show up for each other, the same way we always have.

Chuuk needs us: Super Typhoon Sinlaku

Families in Chuuk lost homes, clean water, and food. AMC stands with our Micronesian neighbors. Monetary donations: Mike Pangelinan · m.pangelinan@cann.us (CANN-A) or Pastor Sato Suka · ssuka@momusa.org (MOMUSA).
